About Me

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), with interests in machine learning, Internet of Things, embedded systems, computer vision, signal processing, renewable energy, and power systems.

My academic and project experience includes IoT-based monitoring systems, autonomous medicine dispensing robotics, real-time drowsiness detection using deep learning, intelligent lighting control, and hardware-assisted malware detection using machine learning. I aim to pursue graduate research that applies intelligent sensing, embedded systems, and data-driven methods to real-world engineering problems in automation, smart energy, healthcare technology, and robotics.
