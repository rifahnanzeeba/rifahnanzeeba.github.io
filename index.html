About Me

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), with research interests in machine learning, Internet of Things, embedded systems, computer vision, signal processing, renewable energy, and power systems.

My academic and project experience includes IoT-based monitoring systems, autonomous medicine dispensing robotics, intelligent lighting control, real-time drowsiness detection using deep learning, and hardware-assisted malware detection using machine learning.

I aim to pursue graduate research that applies intelligent sensing, embedded systems, and data-driven methods to real-world engineering challenges in automation, smart energy, healthcare technology, agriculture, and robotics.
